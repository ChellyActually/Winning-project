The Rock, Paper and Scissors Game

How to play

  • Choose Your Move: Select Rock, Paper, or Scissors to begin the round.
  • The Rules: Rock crushes Scissors, Scissors cuts Paper, and Paper covers Rock.
  • The Computer's Turn: The computer will make a simultaneous, randomized choice.
  • Winning & Losing: If your move beats the computer's, you win the round! If they match, it's a draw.
  • Keep Score: The game tracks your wins and losses—see how many times you can outsmart the algorithm!
